🔔 Booking Status

Pause new customer bookings (e.g. while hiring more technicians) without taking the site offline — unlike Maintenance Mode below, the whole site (SEO pages, careers) stays fully live and searchable, so your Google ranking keeps growing and technicians can still apply, while customers just see a "not accepting bookings" notice instead of the form.

🛠️ Site Maintenance Mode

Turn this on if the site is broken, being updated, or otherwise needs to go offline temporarily. Visitors will see a friendly notice instead of the normal site. The Super Admin and Technician panels stay accessible either way. ⚠️ For anything longer than a couple of days (e.g. "paused until we hire more technicians"), use Booking Status above instead — leaving the whole site down for weeks hurts your Google ranking and stops new technicians from applying.

📈 Protects your Google ranking — tells search engines exactly how soon to check back instead of assuming the page is gone. Keep this realistic (and the actual downtime short) — Google may start dropping pages from search results if a site stays down for several days straight.

Notifications

Automatically send a booking confirmation by SMS and/or WhatsApp the moment a customer's booking is confirmed

How this works

This uses MSG91 — the same service already used for OTP on this site — which also sends SMS and WhatsApp Business messages. Both channels are off by default and safe to leave off; turn on the ones you want once you have the details below from your MSG91 account. Nothing here can break bookings — if a message fails to send, the booking still goes through normally.

  • SMS needs: your MSG91 AuthKey, a DLT-registered SMS Template (India requires this for all transactional SMS), and your approved Sender ID — all set up from your MSG91 dashboard.
  • WhatsApp needs: your MSG91 AuthKey, a WhatsApp Business number connected via MSG91, and a Meta/WhatsApp-approved message template name.
  • Both messages will include: customer name, Booking ID, visit date & time slot, and total amount.

Time Slots

Slot Capacity (auto-control)

Max Jobs Per Technician Per Day (auto-control)

Site-wide default cap on how many bookings one technician can be assigned on the same date, so they don't get overloaded and delay customers. Leave blank for no limit. Commission Settings

Commission Rate

What does this do?

The shared default, charged per completed order. Waived entirely once Admin/Sub-Admin confirms the customer left a Google review for that order (a technician's own claim alone does not waive it — see the Commission report below).

💡 A fixed ₹ amount is a much bigger cut on a low-price job (e.g. RO service) than a high-price one (e.g. AC). Percentage mode keeps the cut proportional — use whichever fits how you think about it.

Per-Appliance Rates (optional)

What does this do?

Give a specific appliance its own commission rate (flat ₹ or %) — e.g. an AC repair might reasonably carry a different rate than a quick RO filter change. Leave a field blank to use the shared default above for that appliance. (A technician's own personal rate below still takes priority over this.)

Per-Technician Rates (optional)

What does this do?

Give a specific technician their own commission rate (flat ₹ or %) — takes priority over everything above, for every job they do. Leave a field blank to use the appliance rate (or the shared default) for that technician instead. Enter 0 specifically to mean they never owe commission, ever.

🔒 Payment Action PIN

What does this do?

Require a 4-digit PIN before "Mark Paid" or "Undo" below can be used — protects real payment records from an accidental tap or from anyone with Admin access who shouldn't be touching them. Separate from your Admin login password.
